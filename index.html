❄️ Ice-Calm Pressure Training

1 — Timer Lock

Safe school questions under a firm timer. Accuracy first.

2 — Distraction Control

Normal background noise. Drift → “target” → return.

3 — Error Recovery

STOP → identify → correct → continue.

4 — Decision Switching

Alternate safe tasks every 60–90 seconds without losing accuracy.

5 — Mock Pressure

Once weekly max, 15–20 minutes. Record accuracy, biggest mistake, adaptation.

Hard limits: no sleep deprivation, pain, frightening yourself, breath-holding contests, extreme cold/heat, or dangerous stunts.
